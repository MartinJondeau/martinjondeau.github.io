Private Equity — Advanced
The LBO: Leveraged Buyout
A leveraged buyout uses debt to acquire a company — typically 60–70% debt, 30–40% equity. The target's cash flows service the debt. As debt pays down, equity value grows mechanically.
Key takeaway: Leverage amplifies equity returns by reducing the equity contribution required at entry.
